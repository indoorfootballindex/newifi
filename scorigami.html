FAQ

What is Scorigami?

Scorigami is a concept created by Jon Bois. It tracks every unique final score combination that has ever occurred. Because of the unusual scoring structure of indoor football — where high-scoring games are common — there are hundreds of theoretically possible scorelines that have never actually happened.

How do I read the chart?

The winning score runs along the top (horizontal axis) and the losing score runs down the left side (vertical axis). Amber cells have happened — brighter means more occurrences. Dark cells have never happened. Black cells are impossible (the loser can't outscore the winner). Click any cell to see the games for that score.

Where does the data come from?

Scores are compiled from dozens of indoor and arena football leagues spanning 1987 to the present, pulled from the same schedule archive used across the rest of the site.
