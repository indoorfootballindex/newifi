Franchise history

The San Diego Strike Force began play in 2019 as an IFL expansion team, taking the field for the first time under owner Roy Choi and general manager Ryan Eucker. That inaugural campaign was rough, a 1–13 finish for the young franchise. The pandemic hit next.

The Strike Force opened the 2020 season with a 50–36 road win over the Bismarck Bucks, but the IFL postponed the rest of the schedule in March 2020 and canceled it outright in April, leaving San Diego at 1–0. Continued pandemic restrictions in California then forced the team to withdraw from the 2021 season entirely, putting the franchise on ice for two full years. That hiatus became a turning point.

During the layoff, ownership transitioned to a new group led by Taylor Genuser, and the team used the downtime to rebuild from the ground up — a complete rebrand including new uniforms and logos that gave the Strike Force the identity it carries today. San Diego returned to the field in 2022 under Genuser with a more offensive-minded approach. The results took a couple years to arrive, but the payoff has been real: in 2024 the Strike Force posted their first winning regular season record at 10–6, made the playoffs for the first time, and picked up their first postseason win before falling in the conference semifinals.

They backed it up in 2025, going 10–6 again to reach the playoffs and reaching the Western Confenece Championship. They fell to the Vegas Knight Hawks, but went 13-3 in 2026, earning the #1 seed in the West. They would fall in the first round to the champs-to-be, the Arizona Rattlers.
